# 01 — Marketing strategy for the Kiwi Go hero Reel

Owner: performance-marketing strategy. Input for the script, shot list and compositing agents.
Sources: `CONTEXT.md`, `research/site/index.html.txt`, `support.html.txt`, `cargo.html.txt`, plus light web research (listed at the end).
All on-screen copy is in Russian. Everything here respects the public rules in CONTEXT.md: no city names as text, no delivery-time promises, no competitor names, never «Скоро запуск».

---

## 0. The verdict in one paragraph

The last film failed because it sold *scenery*. This Reel must sell **relief**. Sending a parcel to another city in Kyrgyzstan is still a trip you make yourself: you go to the bus station, look for a driver, photograph his number plate, and then answer «где посылка?» all evening. Kiwi Go turns that trip into a tap. So the Reel is a tiny cinematic story with one turn in it: **the parcel stops being your errand and becomes a message you sent.** We keep the reference's craft (miniature world, few shots, light-leak cuts, golden grade, the logo born inside the world). The difference is that every shot now carries one benefit, and text arrives from second 0 instead of second 13.

## 1. Audience and priority

### 1A. PRIMARY: online sellers on Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram (about 75% of creative weight)

**Who:** small shops of 1–5 people, often a woman running the shop from home or a small showroom. Clothes, cosmetics, children's goods, home goods, handmade items, phone accessories. Orders come in through DMs and payment is often cash on delivery (наложка). Customers are spread across the regions, and the sellers are mostly in the big city (today Kiwi Go's sending shops are in Bishkek). They post Reels themselves, so they are fluent in the format and judge production quality instantly.

**What they use today, and why it hurts:**

| Current way | Why sellers use it | Why it frustrates |
|---|---|---|
| «Передать с водителем»: a marshrutka, intercity minibus or bus from the bus station | Cheap, leaves today, no paperwork | You have to go there yourself, often in the evening or early morning; the price is «договорная»; the driver doesn't answer; there is no tracking; nobody is responsible if the parcel is damaged or lost; the recipient has to meet the vehicle |
| Taxi «Бишкек—Ош» or a ride-share driver | Fast, flexible | Same lack of control, plus price depends on size or the driver's mood; for COD the seller has to trust the driver with cash |
| State post | Wide network, «официально» | Queue at the branch, recipient must go to the post office, tracking is weak (local press has published reader complaints that parcel movement can't be traced), slow for a seller who promised «завтра-послезавтра» |
| Courier and express services | Proper service | Often price by weight, aimed at documents, unclear COD payout, a percentage of the goods' value |
| A relative or acquaintance who is travelling | Free | Unpredictable, awkward, impossible to scale |

**Their pains, in their own words** (spoken Russian as heard in KG seller chats; use these as tone reference and as optional hook lines):
- «Каждый вечер — на автовокзал с пакетами.»
- «Ищу, кто едет, фоткаю номер машины, скидываю клиентке.»
- «Клиентка пишет: „где моя посылка?“ — а я сама не знаю.»
- «Водитель трубку не берёт.»
- «Посылку помяли, а спросить не с кого.»
- «Цена — как договоришься. Коробка побольше — уже дороже.»
- «Наложку жду неделю, деньги приходится выбивать.»
- «Из регионов заказывают, а доставку объяснить не могу — теряю клиента.»
- «Полдня уходит не на продажи, а на отправки.»

**The underlying job:** "I want to sell to the whole country without becoming a logistics company myself." The real currency is **time and nerves**, then trust with clients, then money.

**What makes them act:** a predictable price («за место», weight doesn't matter), not leaving the house (pickup from the door), visible status to stop the DM storm, and COD money arriving fast (next working day). The conversion action is **connecting the shop**: app «Kiwi Go для магазинов» or app.kiwigo.kg.

### 1B. SECONDARY: private senders (about 25% of creative weight)

**Who:** people sending to family in another city. Gifts and treats, documents, medicine, clothes for a child studying in another city, a forgotten charger, something for a toy (той), seasonal food. Parents and older relatives sit on both ends.

**What they use today:** «передать с водителем» or a fellow traveller, friends who are travelling, sometimes the post.

**Their pains, in their own words:**
- «Стоишь на автовокзале, ищешь, кто едет в ту сторону.»
- «Маме передать лекарства — а как, с кем?»
- «Папа в пять утра поехал встречать маршрутку.»
- «Попросила знакомых — неудобно, и всё равно переживаю.»
- «Звоню водителю каждый час: доехал? отдал?»

**The underlying job:** "I want it to arrive safely without making my parents run around." The emotion is **care and peace of mind**. That is why the SMS code on handover (it reached the right hands) and door delivery to the recipient matter more here than price.

### Priority rule for the Reel
The story is told through the **seller** (she is the one with a repeat problem, she converts into recurring revenue, and she shares Reels with seller peers). The **recipient moment** at the end (door, SMS code, smile) gives private senders their emotional hook for free, without a second storyline.

> Research note: web sources on inter-city parcel pains in KG are thin and mostly anecdotal (classifieds with «цена договорная», ride-share groups, reader complaints about postal tracking). The pain list above combines that with category knowledge. Validate it cheaply before the paid push: 5 DMs to Bishkek Instagram sellers, plus one poll in Stories («Как вы отправляете в регионы?»).

## 2. Core insight and single-minded proposition

**Insight.** *For a seller in Kyrgyzstan, the sale ends in the chat, but the work starts there: every inter-city parcel turns into a trip, a hunt for a driver and an evening of «где посылка?». She doesn't hate paying for delivery. She hates that delivery is her job.*

**Single-minded proposition (the ONE thing to remember):**

> **«Отправить посылку — как написать сообщение.»**

This is the company's own positioning, and it is the right one: it contrasts with the bus-station errand, it is native to people who run their business in messengers, and it can be shown visually (the parcel "sends" itself the way a message does). The proof points below are the **reasons to believe**, not extra messages. If a viewer remembers only one more thing, it should be the price: **«от 350 сом»**.

**What the Reel is NOT about:** cargo to Russia or Wildberries, delivery within a city, couriers as heroes, speed in hours, or a list of 80 destinations.

## 4. Hook strategy for the first 1.5 s

Principles:
1. **Frame 1 must already be "the problem" or "the impossible thing".** No logo and no scenic establishing shot (that was the old film's sin).
2. **Silent-first.** Most Reels are watched with sound off, and our export has no music anyway. The hook line is a burned-in super, at most 5 words, in the upper-middle safe zone (avoid the bottom 20% UI and the right-side buttons).
3. **The miniature look is itself a pattern interrupt** in a feed of talking heads. Keep it, and make the miniature *about the viewer's life*.
4. The super appears **on frame 1** (no fade-in), so the viewer reads it before deciding to scroll.

### Ranked hooks

| # | Visual (first 1.5 s) | Line (super) | Mechanism | Why this rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Tilt-shift miniature: a tiny seller sits on a tiny sofa among boxes at night. Above her (composited), messenger-style bubbles pop in one after another: «где посылка?», «уже отправили?», «??». The bubbles are our own UI, not a real app brand | «Где моя посылка?» | **Relatable pain + native-UI pattern interrupt.** Chat bubbles read instantly as "DMs", the exact moment every seller lives through daily. The miniature makes it charming, not whiny | Hits the primary audience in under a second, sets up the "parcel = message" payoff, and is cheap to execute (bubbles are composited, AI only draws the room) |
| **2** | Miniature bus station at blue dawn: rows of unbranded minibuses, a tiny figure holding a box, looking left and right; the camera pushes in | «Опять на автовокзал?» | **Relatable pain / recognition** («это же я»). Every KG viewer knows this scene | Pure local truth, very strong for both audiences. Slightly slower to read than #1 because it needs scene recognition |
| **3** | A parcel on a doorstep lifts off and streaks into the sky with a soft whoosh trail like a "sent" message, leaving a light leak | «Посылка ушла. Как сообщение.» | **Curiosity gap + visual impossibility.** "Wait, how did the box fly?" | The most brand-ownable shot and it states the proposition. Weaker on pain, so better as the turn of the story than as the opener. Use as A/B variant |
| **4** | A giant real hand places two boxes, one tiny and one big, side by side on a miniature scale; the needle doesn't move. Composited price tag over both | «Вес не важен. Цена одна.» | **Counter-intuitive claim / price shock** (it breaks the mental model "price by kg") | Very effective for sellers of bulky goods (clothes, home). Rational, and works best as a retargeting cut |
| **5** | Scale break: a full-size hand reaches into a miniature Kyrgyz town and puts a real box on a tiny doorstep | «Посылка в другой город?» | **Scale pattern interrupt + direct question** that self-selects the audience | Visually stunning but costly to get right in AI (hand–miniature interaction) |
| **6** | Miniature: a seller asleep under a blanket; through the window, tiny truck headlights wind along a mountain road | «Вы спите — посылка едет.» | **Emotional curiosity / relief.** Promises rest without promising a time | Beautiful and on-reference, but the pain is implicit. Best for the warm brand cut |
| **7** | Close-up of a doorbell or door in a miniature apartment block; the door opens, a courier's hands receive a box | «Заберём прямо у двери.» | **Benefit-first, instant clarity** | Clear but "ad-like" as an opener. Lower stop power, high clarity |
| **8** | Huge «350» in lime, punched over a dark miniature mountain road with a tiny truck | «от 350 сом» | **Number anchor / bold claim** | Works for warm audiences who already know the category. Cold viewers don't yet care. Keep for retargeting |

**Recommendation:** build the master on **Hook 1**, and render **Hook 2** and **Hook 3** as alternative first 1.5–2 s heads on the same body (cheap: one extra clip each). Test all three in the first 48–72 h of paid distribution, then keep the winner by 3-second hold rate.

## 5. Story structure (target 20 s, flexible 15–25 s)

Format: 9:16, 24 fps, miniature/tilt-shift cinematic, one golden grade with bloom, **5 shots plus end card**, light-leak cuts, and one camera move that doubles as a transition (as in the reference). There is no music in the export, so the beat points below are marked for the client's music editor.

Arc: **Hook → Pain → Turn → Journey/Proof → Arrival → Brand + CTA**

| Time | Beat | Picture (AI, no text) | Super (composited, Russian) | Music beat marker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | **Hook / pain** | Miniature room at night, seller among boxes, phone glow | Bubbles pop: «где посылка?» «уже отправили?»; main super **«Где моя посылка?»** | Soft start; bubble pops can be on 1/8 notes |
| 2.0–4.5 | **Pain, escalated** | Miniature bus station at dawn, tiny figure with box among minibuses (or a quick push-in) | «Опять на автовокзал?» | Tension, low |
| 4.5–5.2 | **Light leak (orange) = the turn** | — | — | **Beat 1: hit / riser end** |
| 5.2–8.5 | **Transformation** | Warm evening doorstep: the seller hands a box to a courier (back to camera, unbranded jacket in lime or ink tones). Optional: the box "sends" with a soft light trail | **«Заберём у двери»** | Music opens up |
| 8.5–13.0 | **Journey + proof** | Unbranded truck (clean blank side panel for later compositing) on a miniature mountain road, night to golden dawn; the camera **cranes up** over the ridge (the move is the transition) | Status chips tick in one by one, top-left: «Забрали» → «В пути» → «В городе» → «У курьера»; main super **«Видно каждый шаг»** | Chips land on beats; **Beat 2: swell on the crane-up** |
| 13.0–16.0 | **Arrival / proof** | Recipient opens an apartment door in a miniature regional town; takes the box; smiles. A phone in hand (screen blank green for compositing) | «Код из SMS — и посылка у вас»; small line: «Наложка — на следующий рабочий день» | Warm resolve |
| 16.0–17.0 | **Logo born in the world** | The truck's blank wheel or a round sun reflection lines up where we composite the kiwi-wheel logo (the logo itself is ours, never AI) | — | **Beat 3: peak** |
| 17.0–20.0 | **End card** (paper #FFFDF7) | Logo icon, then wordmark types on | **«Посылка — как сообщение»** / **«от 350 сом · цена за место»** / CTA line (see §6) | Hold, tail |
| 20.0–21.0 | Flare to black (optional) | — | — | Tail |

**Why this isn't a boring ad:** there is one character and one parcel, and we follow it. The proof points are woven into the picture (pickup, status chips, SMS code) instead of read off a list. At most **one super per shot**, with the exception of the chips. The price appears once, at the end, where it rewards attention.

**Shorter 15 s cut:** drop the bus station beat (2.0–4.5) and shorten the journey to 3 s.

**Production notes for the downstream agents:**
- The kiwi-slice wheels on the website truck ARE the logo idea. Do not let AI draw kiwi wheels. Use plain wheels in AI, and if we want the kiwi wheel, composite our SVG onto it.
- The courier and truck must stay unbranded or blank-panelled. Keep the lime #B8F03C as a wardrobe or prop accent so the brand colour lives inside the world before the logo appears.
- Font: **Onest** (matches kiwigo.kg, excellent Cyrillic, friendlier than Inter). Supers in Onest SemiBold, ink #111410 on a paper/cream pill, or paper on an ink pill; lime only as an accent (a dot, an underline, the active status chip).

## 6. CTA options (Russian)

Real conversion actions: order or connect via **app.kiwigo.kg**, write in **WhatsApp**, **follow** the account, and **shop connects** to «Kiwi Go для магазинов».

**End-card CTA (pick one primary, max one secondary line):**
1. **«Оформите доставку — app.kiwigo.kg»** (primary, seller and private)
2. «Подключите магазин — app.kiwigo.kg» (seller-targeted cut)
3. «Напишите нам в WhatsApp» (paired with the WhatsApp icon; best for private senders and paid ads with a WhatsApp destination)
4. «Первая доставка — в пару кликов»
5. «Работает в браузере — без установки»

**In-caption or comment CTAs (for organic):**
- «Ссылка в профиле — оформите первую доставку» (link in bio is how IG organic converts)
- «Напишите „ХОЧУ“ в комментарии — пришлём, как подключить магазин» (comment-trigger, boosts engagement signals; needs someone or an auto-DM to answer)
- «Сохраните, чтобы не искать водителя в следующий раз»
- «Отправьте подруге, которая каждый вечер ездит на автовокзал» (share prompt aimed at sellers)
- «Подпишитесь — покажем, как отправлять без лишних звонков»

**Paid placement:** use the CTA button «Отправить сообщение» (WhatsApp destination) for private senders, and «Подробнее» → app.kiwigo.kg for shops.

## 7. Russian copy bank

Rules: headlines are 4–6 words max, supers are 2–5 words, sentence case, «ё» kept where it belongs, guillemets « » for quotes, «сом» (not «сомов») after numbers, no city names, no time promises, no «Скоро».

### Headlines / end-card lines (4–6 words)
- **Отправить посылку — как сообщение** (hero)
- Посылка в другой город? Легко
- Посылки между городами Кыргызстана
- Без автовокзала и без звонков
- Заберём у двери, привезём к двери
- Вы продаёте — мы довезём
- Цена за место, вес не важен
- Посылку видно на каждом шаге
- Продавайте по всей стране спокойно *(note: "по всей стране" describes reach of sales, not a coverage promise; drop it if legal prefers)*

### Supers (in-shot)
- «Где моя посылка?»
- «Опять на автовокзал?»
- «Заберём у двери»
- «Видно каждый шаг»
- «Без звонков „где посылка“»
- «Код из SMS — и посылка у вас»
- «Наложка — на следующий рабочий день»
- «Платите только за доставку»
- «от 350 сом»
- «Цена за место»
- «От двери до двери»

### Status chips (composited UI, journey beat)
«Забрали» · «В пути» · «В городе» · «У курьера» · «Вручено»

### Chat bubbles (Hook 1)
«где посылка?» · «уже отправили?» · «а когда будет?» · «??» (lower-case, as people really type)

### Caption draft (organic post)
> Посылка в другой город — без автовокзала и звонков водителю.
> Заберём у двери, отвезём между городами Кыргызстана и вручим по коду из SMS. Каждый шаг видно в приложении.
> от 350 сом за место — вес и размер цену не меняют.
> Для магазинов: наложка — на следующий рабочий день.
> Оформить: app.kiwigo.kg или WhatsApp (ссылка в профиле).

### Banned or avoid
«Скоро запуск», «доставим завтра», «за ночь», «до обеда», «быстрее всех», «№1», any city names in text, competitor names, «по всему Кыргызстану» (over-claim), «получатель тоже видит статус» (no recipient tracking page yet), «бесплатно».

## 8. Success metrics and what drives each

Targets are for the first 7 days, paid plus organic, from a new account. Treat them as decision thresholds, not guarantees.

| Metric | Target | Creative driver | If it underperforms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3-second hold** (3 s views / impressions) | ≥ 35% organic, ≥ 25% paid (thumb-stop ratio) | Hook frame + super on frame 1; recognisable pain (chat bubbles, bus station); miniature look as pattern interrupt; no logo up front | Swap in Hook 2 or 3; make the super larger and shorter; start mid-action |
| **Completion rate / avg. watch %** | ≥ 25% full plays at 20 s; avg. watch ≥ 60% | One character, one parcel, one question carried to the end («where is the parcel?» is resolved only at the door); light-leak cuts with no dead air; the price is held back to the end card | Cut to the 15 s version; remove the bus station beat; tighten the journey |
| **Shares / sends** | ≥ 1% of reach | Seller in-jokes («где посылка?», «опять на автовокзал») that people send to seller friends; the share-prompt caption | Lean the bubbles more into humour; add a more specific seller line |
| **Saves** | ≥ 1% of reach | Useful, keep-for-later facts: price per place, COD next working day, the step chips; the caption as a mini-guide | Pin a carousel or Story with «Как отправить: 3 шага» |
| **Profile visits** | ≥ 1.5% of reach | Curiosity about the brand (a beautiful world + a clear "who is this"); the logo born inside the scene; the handle on the end card | Show the logo earlier (at the arrival beat) |
| **Link clicks** (bio / CTA / WhatsApp) | CTR ≥ 0.8% paid; ≥ 0.3% of reach organic | One clear CTA with app.kiwigo.kg; price anchor «от 350 сом» just before it; «работает в браузере» lowers friction | Test the WhatsApp CTA vs the app CTA; add the price to the hook (Hook 8) for retargeting |
| **Business outcome** | Shops registered and first orders attributed (UTM `?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=hero_reel` on app.kiwigo.kg; a separate WhatsApp opener «Здравствуйте! Я из Reels») | The seller-first story | — |

**Measurement setup:** a separate UTM per hook variant; a pre-filled WhatsApp text per variant; and a note of follower delta and DMs mentioning the Reel. Decide on the winning hook after about 3,000 impressions per variant.
