# 05 — Independent marketing and creative review of the three concepts

Reviewer role: head of strategy, pre-client review. I did not write 01–04. Written 2026-11-18.
Read: `CONTEXT.md`, `01-marketing-strategy.md`, `03-reels-craft.md` (§2 critique, §4 checklist), `04-concepts.md`. I checked the claims against the public site text in `research/site/index.html.txt` and `support.html.txt`.
Nothing was generated and no credits were spent.

---

## 0. The short version

- **None of the three concepts can go to the client as written.** Each one has a single fatal flaw:
  - **C1** has the best story, but its proposition never appears on screen and its brand moment is a sticker on a box.
  - **C2** has the best idea, but a viewer can't tell what the company does until 8 s, and its logo-in-the-sky ending repeats the old film.
  - **C3** has the best craft fit, but it has no protagonist, opens on scenery (the old film's sin) and carries two cultural landmines: the tunduk and a yurt in the courtyard.
- **The winner is a hybrid built on C1's spine.** It takes C1's seller story, C2's "sent like a message" device (only as a UI bubble and a light trail, no levitating box) and C3's composited kiwi-wheel truck as the logo moment. Details are in §2.
- **Two claim problems appear in every concept:**
  - Coverage variant A («по всему Кыргызстану») must be deleted, not offered to the client as an option.
  - The arrival shots show a Kiwi Go courier delivering in a village or regional courtyard. The support page says Kiwi Go delivers in Osh and **partner services** handle the regions, often only «до центра» or «у дороги».
- Verdict: **conditional GO for the hybrid. NO-GO for C2 or C3 as the master.**

### 1.2 Why each score

**1. Hook (sound off, 1.5 s)**
- **C1 = 7.** The relatable pain is right for sellers. But frame 0 carries a 40 % fog, four bubbles popping, and a pill super that **repeats bubble 1 word for word** («где посылка?» / «Где моя посылка?»). That is five text elements in 1.5 s on a tiny figure. The motion is a slow push and an exhale, so it is low-energy. "Sad woman on sofa" is also a common ad opener.
- **C2 = 7.** The impossible image is the strongest stopper on paper. As specified, though, the box is only "2 cm off the ground, rising slowly" under 40 % fog. At phone size in a miniature, 2 cm reads as a box on a step. It only becomes a hook if the box is visibly in the air on frame 0.
- **C3 = 6.** A truck on a mountain road at golden hour is beautiful, but it is the **same genre as the old film's opener** (a mountain path and an aerial). The question super does select the audience, but the picture carries no pain and no impossibility.

**2. Clarity by 5 s**
- **C1 = 6.** By 4.25 s we get pain, then a courier at the door with «Заберём у двери». "Courier picks up" is clear. "**Between cities**" is not stated until the card (descriptor) or by implication at 9 s. The brand name is not seen until 14 s.
- **C2 = 5.** The box flies off by itself, and then the super says «Заберём у двери» over a phone shot. **The picture contradicts the claim**, because nobody picks anything up. The author admits the service is unclear until the courier appears at 8 s.
- **C3 = 7.** The question, a truck and a door pickup are all on screen by 2–4 s, and «Kiwi Go для магазинов» appears on the app card at 3.5 s. It is the clearest on "what", but weakest on "why me".

**3. Single-minded proposition**
- **C1 = 5.** It claims «как сообщение» as the SMP, but that line **never appears on screen** in C1. The card descriptor is the coverage line. On screen it is six supers plus a five-element card, which makes it a feature list in a nice coat.
- **C2 = 9.** The proposition is the physics of the film and is stated at frame 0. It loses a point for still stacking coverage, tracking and SMS supers.
- **C3 = 4.** «Посылки между городами — от двери до двери» is a category description that any courier could sign. The real proposition is relegated to "tagline backup".

**4. Proof and credibility**
- **C1 = 7.** Pickup, seller-side tracking on *her* phone, SMS handover and price are all shown in action, which is the most honest set. It loses points for showing the Kiwi Go courier delivering at a regional house (see §4).
- **C2 = 5.** Magic undermines credibility for a category whose buyers fear loss and damage. A flying box is the opposite of "your parcel is in safe hands". The courier at the end only partly repairs this.
- **C3 = 6.** Good proof list. But «Цена за место — вес не важен» is an absolute claim (§4), and the arrival is a village yurt courtyard served by "our" courier, which over-states the network.

**5. Emotion and story**
- **C1 = 8.** It is the only real before/after (same sofa, cold to warm). It loses points because the protagonist vanishes for 7 s and the payoff is a stranger hugging a box plus a tea sip (stock "relief").
- **C2 = 6.** Wonder, but no arc. The seller is a spectator, and the elder's nod is generic.
- **C3 = 4.** There is no human stake: a toy truck travels, and the people are props.

**6. CTA strength** (the same card everywhere, so the scores differ only on audience fit)
- All three have the CTA pill at **17.5 s** of 20 s. With the strategist's own completion target of 25 %, about **three viewers in four never see it**.
- **C1 = 7.** «Подключите магазин» matches the primary audience and the real conversion.
- **C2 = 6** and **C3 = 6.** «Оформите доставку» is aimed partly at private senders, and the support page says **only Bishkek shops send today** (§4). That CTA may lead to a dead end.

**7. Brand distinctiveness**
- **C1 = 5.** A logo on a parcel sticker is what *every* courier's label looks like. The kiwi-wheel, the brand's best asset, is unused. The brand is carried only by a lime cap.
- **C2 = 7.** A lime trail from frame 0 is real colour ownership. The logo in the sky is borrowed from the reference.
- **C3 = 8.** It has the composited kiwi wheels on a truck, which is the site's own illustration. It would be 9, except that the tunduk payoff is unusable (§4).

**8. Fit with the reference style**
- **C1 = 5.** Seven shots, two interiors, phone UI and a bus yard. It is a story ad wearing a miniature grade, not the reference's 3-shot glide.
- **C2 = 8.** A journey, a crane into cloud and the logo in the sky make it near-structurally the reference.
- **C3 = 9.** A diorama, a follow-cam journey and a crane: it is the reference translated to Kyrgyzstan.

**9. AI feasibility**
- **C1 = 5.** The same face across S1, S2, S3, S4 and S6, two handovers, a phone screen and a bus yard full of potential gibberish. It is the riskiest set.
- **C2 = 6.** The levitation has a credit-free sprite fallback, which is good. A handover and a thumb on a phone remain.
- **C3 = 6.** The author says "low risk", but I disagree. The 10 s FLF hero move is the single longest and most melt-prone generation in all three concepts, and its contingency (Cinema Studio at 25 cr per try) shows the author knows it. A top-down crane that must land a crown dead centre is also hard. Its saving grace is that the people are tiny.

**10. Loop and rewatch**
- All three use the cream-fog loop.
- **C1 = 6.** Card → sad seller is a downer restart.
- **C2 = 7.** Card → the parcel lifting again reads as "sent again".
- **C3 = 7.** The diorama detail rewards a second look.

## 2. Winner: hybrid «Отправлено» on C1's spine

**Why C1's spine:** it is the only concept whose protagonist is the buyer, and the only one with a real turn. Seller-first is the strategist's priority rule, and it is where the recurring revenue is.

**What it borrows:**
- **From C2:** the proposition, made visible without levitation. Her tap sends a lime "Отправлено" bubble (the turn). The overnight journey is drawn as a lime long-exposure **light trail along the road**, which is a real truck's headlights rendered like a sent message. Both are composites, so they carry zero AI risk.
- **From C3:** the **kiwi-wheel truck** as the logo born in the world. The wheel *is* the logo, and the site already tells this story ("грузовик с колёсами-киви"). It replaces both C1's mundane sticker and C2's old-film sky logo.
- **Story closure that none of the three has:** the recipient is **the customer who wrote «где посылка?»**, and the last beat is her new bubble «получила, спасибо!» on the seller's screen. The pain bubbles from frame 0 are answered, and that gives the loop its meaning.

**What it drops:**
- the bus yard (it becomes hook variant B)
- the sticker logo
- the sky logo
- the tunduk
- the village or yurt arrival
- coverage variant A

**Projected score** (if executed to the fixes below): hook 8, clarity 8, SMP 8, proof 8, emotion 8, CTA 8, brand 8, reference 7, feasibility 6, loop 8 = **77**. This is a projection, not a result.

**Budget sanity check:**
- It removes C1's S2 and S7 and adds one truck turnaround still.
- It keeps one FLF crane and five K3P shots.
- My estimate from the unit prices in 04 is **about 190–210 cr with contingency**. That is not a quote; the production agent must re-quote with the estimate tools.
- About 280 cr stay in reserve for client revisions.

### 3.2 Specific cuts, merges and reorders against C1 as written

1. **Cut C1 S2 (the bus yard) from the master.** It delays the turn to 4.0 s, adds a 2nd pain beat to a 20 s film, and is the most gibberish-prone plate (route boards and plates). Keep it as hook test **H-B**.
2. **Cut C1 S7 (the sticker logo).** It is a box label, and nobody will remember it as a logo moment. Replace it with S6, the kiwi wheel.
3. **Cut C1 S6's second handover.** Start the shot post-handover.
4. **Merge C1 S4's tracking into the S3 PiP.** It is the same information without a keyed phone screen.
5. **Move the coverage line out of the card into S3.** The card descriptor becomes the proposition.
6. **Replace «Без автовокзала и звонков»** (C1 S5): it is two ideas in one super. The caption can carry it.

### 3.3 Super rules applied (checklist §D)

- Every super is 3–4 words, Onest SemiBold 72 px on a cream pill, and held ≥ 1.65 s, which beats the 0.4 s/word minimum.
- Never more than **one pill plus the PiP** on screen. Bubbles and the PiP never share the screen (S1/S5 vs S3/S4).
- Bubbles sit in y 420–640 and pills at P-HIGH y 380 or P-LOW y 1100. Both stay inside y 270–1250, and the 4:5 crop survives.
- Typography: «Между городами Кыргызстана» with no hyphenation, and a non-breaking space in «350 сом». Every line goes to a **native proofreader** before rendering.

### 4.2 Cultural sensitivities in Kyrgyzstan

1. **Tunduk → logo (C3 S5): kill it, and kill the лепёшка fallback too.**
   - The tunduk is the central emblem of the state flag. The flag was redesigned in 2023 amid public debate, so the symbol is currently emotionally loaded.
   - A top-down tunduk centred on a golden-sun background is visually close to the flag itself. State-symbol rules restrict commercial use of the flag; I am not giving legal advice, and local counsel should confirm the current wording.
   - Even if it is legal, the tunduk is a family heirloom: the yurt crown passes down the generations and stays with the family when the yurt is taken down.
   - Turning it into a delivery company's logo invites a "they turned our heritage into an ad" backlash in the comments, and in a market this small the comments *are* the brand.
   - The fallback is bread (нан), which is treated as sacred. Cross-fading a лепёшка into a fruit-slice logo is a weaker version of the same mistake.
   - The kiwi wheel is the brand's own motif and needs no cultural licence.
2. **A white yurt beside a house in a town courtyard (C3 S4).**
   - To local eyes, a yurt put up in a residential courtyard usually signals a **funeral or memorial** (or a large toy).
   - In an arrival scene with a smiling recipient, it is at best a mixed signal and at worst a grim one. Remove it.
   - Yurts belong in jailoo or lake landscapes only (C3 S3 is fine).
3. **Elders as toy figures** (C1: an older woman in a headscarf; C2: an older man in a kalpak).
   - The kalpak is a national symbol with its own national day. A toy-style aksakal reads as charming only if it is dignified.
   - No comic posing, no exaggerated features, no hugging a box like a prize.
   - The hybrid sidesteps this by making the recipient the young customer.
4. **Language.** Russian is an official language, so a Russian-only Reel is fine for Bishkek sellers.
   - The regions and recipients skew Kyrgyz-speaking, and language rules for advertising have tightened in recent years; check the current requirements.
   - A **Kyrgyz-language super and card track** is a composite-only re-export (zero credits), and it is cheap insurance plus a reach gain. Do it.
5. **Drivers and the bus station (hook H-B).** Show the hassle, not the people. Don't caricature marshrutka drivers; they are many viewers' relatives.
6. **Landmarks.** If the dawn-city plate resembles Sulaiman-Too (Osh, sacred, UNESCO), keep it as background only, with no logo or trail over it.
